MILLEVIZ Seven problems · One million dollars each

The Millennium Prize Problems · Clay Mathematics Institute, 2000

Seven questions mathematics has not answered.

In May 2000 the Clay Mathematics Institute named seven unsolved problems and put a million dollars on each. One has been solved. Six are still open. Every page here starts from a picture you can move, then works up to what the problem actually says.
